When it appears. The app checks on launch and every 6 hours. If a newer version exists, the button shows and modal 1 opens once for that version.
Later vs Skip. Later closes the modal and shows it again on next launch. Skip stops the auto-popup for this version only. The button stays in both cases.
Required updates. For critical fixes, modal 1 has no Later or Skip, a red "Required" tag, and one primary action. It still waits for the person to confirm.
